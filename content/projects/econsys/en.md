# EconSys

EconSys is a modular economic simulation API for online communities, designed to power virtual currencies, rewards, and perks across multiple platforms. It models users, assets, markets, and policies in real time, so you can build rich economies that feel responsive and fair.

## The Journey

The system started life as a proprietary engine behind many of the Discord bots I built for communities that needed ways to reward playtime, activity, and contributions for both regular members and staff. That original version has powered economic systems in over 15 bots I’ve made, many of them free and some commissioned. Across those deployments, it has served roughly 150,000 distinct users in more than 300 servers. One community with over 6,000 active users reported a 12% increase in activity after integrating the system, with noticeable improvements in engagement and player retention.

## Next Steps

I’m now rebuilding EconSys as an open-source API, available for anyone to self-host and extend. The goal is to make it easy for other developers to create bots and services like the ones I used to ship.

As I slowly step back from day-to-day Discord bot development and from creating new economic simulation bots myself, open-sourcing EconSys is my way of handing the tools to others. Instead of the engine staying locked inside a handful of bots, it becomes a shared foundation that anyone can study, adapt, and build on.
